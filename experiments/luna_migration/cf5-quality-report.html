Next scientific gate, not an implemented workaround

A broader study should cross fixed population-generation conditions with fixed response-model conditions using the same concrete draws, survey wording and simulated time. Freeze the conditions and population-sampling seeds before examining outcomes; repeat independent model runs and retain the existing validation thresholds. No unsupported deterministic model-seed parameter is assumed. Unknown recruitment eligibility must remain unknown, rather than assuming an age-25 filter. Quantitative validation needs eligible human comparison data and product-level holdouts, not further tuning to this one gazpacho distribution. Mean, dispersion, ordinal distribution distance and, across multiple products, relative appeal must be assessed together; more varied numbers alone are not an acceptance criterion.

Published survey-method research documents over-precise synthetic responses: Bisbee et al., Synthetic Replacements for Human Survey Data? (2024). An explicitly different measurement protocol is discussed in the preprint Maier et al., LLMs Reproduce Human Purchase Intent via Semantic Similarity Elicitation of Likert Ratings: free-text responses are mapped through embedding similarities to rating distributions. Its 57-survey, 9,300-response personal-care evaluation is not evidence that the method works for Luna or gazpacho. Crucially, the authors state that their reference anchors were manually optimized for those same 57 surveys and that transfer to other surveys remains unclear. Their reported correlation attainment is not individual-response accuracy.

No such measurement change has been implemented. Any future experiment must keep derived probabilities distinct from publicly stated ratings, preserve the original extraction contract, freeze anchors without gazpacho outcome tuning, and test unseen products. Replacing a spoken 2 with an embedding-derived higher score would not repair extraction fidelity or establish behavioral realism.

Conclusion

The factual-retention and extraction repairs are supported by actual end-to-end execution and targeted source-fidelity checks. The parents study is no longer uniformly rejecting the product, but remains repetitive and is not empirically validated. Investment is better grounded, not uniformly better conversationally. Gazpacho still needs a defensible research solution: neither successful execution, higher mean ratings nor more varied answers alone would establish one. A broader population/response calibration design would be a separate scientific and engineering decision, not an undisclosed prompt trick or silent model fallback.
